INSIDE THE LIFE OF A D1 USF WOMEN’S SOCCER ATHLETE

Hello friend! My name is Ella. I am from Seattle Washington and started playing soccer at age 4. From rec league to club, to highschool and finally division 1 college soccer, I have learned A LOT. Now, as a senior playing for the University of San Francisco, I'm looking to give back to the soccer community.

When I first decided I wanted to play collegiate soccer back in high school, I didn’t have much guidance. When I narrowed down what I was looking for, There were no particular websites or blogs explaining what a real day looked like for a college athlete.

Even if I found a small "day in the life" video, it was so short, and even then most videos/websites/blogs were VERY outdated.

Back in 2022, I had to do a lot of research to find the perfect fit. It can be daunting, and stressful- especially not having simple resources to help you make such a big decision.

After 4 here at the USF, I have helped many youth athletes going through the same recruiting process! Through coaching club teams in the bay area, attending alumni q&a zooms at my old club, and 1 on 1 sessions with young athletes, my blog idea can come to life!

By combining all of this content together I hope I can give back to women’s sports and help younger athletes aspiring to play in college. This blog gives a glimpse into my day to day highlighting our essentials to perform well, our recovery/offtime and go-to snacks to help us stay energized through training, matches and school.

Wherever you are in your soccer journey, we all share a love for the game and I hope you can find something useful to help you on your path. Wishing you the best and feel free to reach out to me with any questions or comments!
